How RoadGuard AI Works

A transparent, automated four-step lifecycle from defect discovery to independent repair certification.

📸

1. AI Damage Detection

Detects potholes, alligator cracking, and damaged road shoulders from citizen photos, dashcams, or highway CCTV feeds with bounding boxes and severity estimation.

📋

2. Jurisdiction Matching

Generates a unique tracking ID (RD-YYYY-XXXXXX) and maps coordinates to the responsible agency (NHAI, State PWD, or Municipal Corporation).

🚧

3. Crew Dispatch & Repair

Field engineers conduct physical surveys, assign hot-mix asphalt patching teams, and monitor real-time SLA countdowns to prevent overdue backlog.

✅

4. Independent Verification

Computer-vision before-and-after texture comparison evaluates asphalt leveling. Independent auditors verify restoration before marking complaints resolved.

Multi-Sensor Intelligence Stack

🤖 Computer Vision & YOLO

Extracts asphalt distress contours, classifies pothole geometric depth, and distinguishes between low-risk hairline cracks and structural alligator fatigue.

📹 Highway CCTV Streams

Compatible with RTSP/HTTP feeds across toll plazas and junctions. Performs automated periodic keyframe extraction without streaming private credentials to clients.

🛰️ Geospatial & Satellite Screening

Identifies macroscopic corridor surface washouts and shoulder subsidence across vast rural networks. Micro-distress is then investigated by street-level patrols.
