Satellite & Macroscopic Road Screening

Regional corridor reflectance analysis to screen for road shoulder washouts, base structural subsidence, and severe waterlogging.

⚠️ Scientific & Technical Limitation Notice

Ordinary satellite imagery (0.5m – 10m resolution) cannot reliably identify individual small potholes or hairline cracks. RoadGuard AI applies satellite reflectance indices for macroscopic corridor screening (e.g. embankments eroded by monsoon floods, major road washouts, and extensive pavement dislodgement). Flagged segments are scheduled for localized on-ground verification using CCTV patrol vehicles or citizen mobile uploads. Roads are never marked as defective merely due to cloud shadow or sensor noise.
